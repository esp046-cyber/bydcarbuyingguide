New to electric cars?

EV or plug-in hybrid?

An EV runs only on a battery. You plug it in and never buy petrol. A plug-in hybrid (PHEV) has a battery and a petrol engine, so you can drive on electricity for short trips and use petrol on long ones. DM-i is BYD's plug-in hybrid system. DMO is the off-road version.

What are kWh and range?

kWh is battery size, like a fuel tank. A bigger battery usually means more range. Range is how far the car goes on a full charge. Real-world range is usually lower than the brochure figure.

Where will I charge?

A regular outlet charges slowly. A wall charger is faster. If you live in a condo or village, ask your association before you buy. Public fast chargers are useful for trips but cost more per kWh.

Buying as an OFW

Why importing is risky

The amounts depend on the vehicle's classification and circumstances. UAE and Philippine versions can differ in specifications, cooling systems, software, charging compatibility and local requirements. Ask the dealer for these in writing.

Warranty

Warranty is set by the Philippine distributor. For the Atto 2 and Seal 5 DM-i, the reported terms are 6 years or 160,000 km for the vehicle and 8 years or 160,000 km for the Blade Battery. For an imported car, coverage must be confirmed for the exact vehicle and VIN. See BYD Cars Philippines.

EV number-coding exemption

The DOE said in October 2025, and again in July 2026, that EVs, including plug-in hybrids, are automatically exempt from number coding under EVIDA, with no separate DOE certification. The exemption is reported to run until about 2030. Enforcement confusion has happened before, so ask the dealer to confirm in writing that your exact model qualifies. Read the DOE EV recognition advisory and the Manila Bulletin report.
