5-year running cost

Rates used: PH home ₱13/kWh, PH fast charge ₱25/kWh, petrol ₱62/L. UAE home AED 0.30/kWh, public AED 0.95/kWh, petrol AED 2.99/L. Assumes 15 kWh/100 km for EVs and 5.5 L/100 km for plug-in hybrids. Excludes insurance, maintenance, registration and VAT/tax differences. Perks: PH EVs are exempt from number-coding and have had 0% excise tax; UAE adds 5% VAT, with Salik and green parking perks varying by emirate. Check current rules.

Shipping a UAE-spec BYD to the Philippines

Usually not worth it. Reasons:

  • Customs: Bureau of Customs duties plus VAT can erase any savings from a cheaper UAE price.
  • Warranty: Local dealer warranty, including the Blade Battery cover, applies to cars sold by the Philippine distributor. A parallel import may lose it.
  • Charging and software: Connector, telematics and app differences may apply to GCC-spec cars.
  • Climate tuning: GCC cars are calibrated for desert heat, not Philippine humidity and flooding.
  • Parts and service: The local network stocks parts for local-spec cars.

Better option: compare local prices and ask for OFW or balikbayan promos. Confirm the rules with a licensed customs broker and the dealer.
